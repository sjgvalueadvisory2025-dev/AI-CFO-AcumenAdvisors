05 — Questions accelerators ask first

The first 8 questions
everyone asks.

How do we know the CFO advice is actually good?

Every report is reviewed and signed off by a senior CFO — operators who've spent years inside funds and financial institutions. Happy to share anonymised samples on a call.

What about founder data privacy?

All data reviewed under NDA-equivalent terms, never used to train models, deleted on the founder's request. We share only aggregated, anonymised statistics with you.

What if one of our founders complains?

Escalation to a partner within 4 hours. We refund any paid product on request, no questions asked. We treat your reputation as our reputation.

We already have a bookkeeper partner. Does this overlap?

No — bookkeepers do bookkeeping. We do CFO-level analysis: runway, unit economics, scenario planning, investor narrative. Happy to coordinate with your bookkeeping partner on any specific founder.

Will you spam our founders?

One email when the Snapshot is delivered. One follow-up at 7 days if they haven't engaged. After that, nothing unless they ask. You set the cadence rules.

Can we just try this with one cohort first?

Yes. Tier 1 is designed for exactly that — listed perk, no commitment, no revenue share. We earn the right to Tier 2 by delivering well in Tier 1.

How long does setup take?

Tier 1: one business day. Tier 2: three business days (we build your custom co-branded landing page). Tier 3: one week (we coordinate the launch event).

What's the catch?

No catch. We can offer this economically because ~5–10% of partnership founders eventually convert to ongoing fractional-CFO retainers — that's where our real revenue sits. Everything upstream of that is a fair-priced lead-magnet for both sides.
