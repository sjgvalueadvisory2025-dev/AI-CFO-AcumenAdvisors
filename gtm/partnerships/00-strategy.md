# Accelerator Partnership Strategy

> Why partnerships are the single highest-leverage channel for Acumen
> Advisors AI-CFO — and the operating thesis for every conversation.

---

## 1. The thesis in one paragraph

Accelerators promise their founders **access** — to capital, to mentors,
and to a "perks marketplace" of free or discounted services. Almost none
of them offer CFO-level help, because real CFOs cost $200k/year and don't
do free work for 60 portfolio companies. Acumen's AI-CFO is the **first
CFO-level service that fits inside a perks-marketplace economic model**:
free Snapshot for every portfolio founder, discounted Deep Dive, full
Founder Call available. The accelerator gets a perk worth bragging about;
the founder gets a real artifact in 24 hours; Acumen gets ~30-100 highly
qualified inbound Snapshots per partnership, of which 15–25% convert paid.

## 2. Why this beats every other channel

| Channel                              | Cost per Snapshot | Conversion to paid | 30-day yield (10 paid) |
|--------------------------------------|-------------------|---------------------|------------------------|
| Cold email (1,200 sends)             | ~$0.20            | 12–18%              | 3–4 paid                |
| LinkedIn DM (600 DMs)                | ~$1.00            | 18–25%              | 2–3 paid                |
| X reply-guy (300 touches)            | ~$0.10            | 15–20%              | 1–2 paid                |
| Founder communities (12 posts)       | ~$0.05            | 10–15%              | 1–2 paid                |
| Organic content (20 posts)           | ~$0.50            | 20–30%              | 1–2 paid                |
| **Accelerator partnership**          | **~$0**           | **25–35%**          | **3–6 paid PER partnership** |
| Bookkeeper referral                  | ~$0               | 30–40%              | 1–2 paid                |

**One signed accelerator partnership = the same yield as 600 cold emails
+ 300 LinkedIn DMs combined**, at a fraction of the work, with
materially higher conversion (because the founder shows up trusting the
brand).

## 3. The two-sided value exchange

For the partnership to be durable, **both** sides need a real win.

### What the accelerator/VC partner gets

1. **A "CFO-on-demand" perk** they can list on their perks marketplace
   alongside AWS credits, Stripe Atlas, etc. — visibly upgrading their
   portfolio support
2. **Better-prepared founders at their Investor Days / Demo Days** —
   founders walking into the pitch with a CFO-reviewed financial story
   close more rounds, which makes the accelerator's track record better
3. **Zero operational lift** — Acumen handles intake, delivery, and
   support. The partner just sends one email or posts in one Slack channel
4. **A talking point in their next fundraising pitch to LPs**: "We provide
   CFO-level financial diligence support to every portco at no cost"
5. **Optional revenue share** on paid upgrades (we'll offer 15-20% on
   Deep Dive + Founder Call — explicitly negotiable)

### What Acumen gets

1. A warm, trusted-brand inbound channel that delivers 30-100 free
   Snapshots per partnership (high-intent, fully-qualified by the
   accelerator's own ICP filter)
2. Social proof — "Trusted by Antler / Blume / Surge portfolio founders"
   on the homepage by Day 30
3. The retainer-customer pipeline: ~5-10% of partnership founders become
   ongoing fractional CFO retainer customers at $2-8k/month — the real
   business

## 4. Partnership tiers

We pre-build three tiers so every conversation is concrete. Lead with
Tier 2 ("Partner"); fall back to Tier 1 if they hesitate; offer Tier 3
to the warmest, biggest-name partner first.

### Tier 1 — Listed Perk ("the easy yes")

- Acumen listed on partner's perks marketplace / founder benefits page
- Every portfolio founder gets: **free Snapshot** + **50% off Deep Dive
  ($49 instead of $99)** + **25% off Founder Call ($261 instead of $349)**
- Acumen delivers all reports
- No revenue share, no co-branding
- **Operational lift on partner: zero**

### Tier 2 — Co-branded Partnership ("the main ask")

- Tier 1 benefits, PLUS:
- A custom landing page on Acumen's site: `/partners/{partner-slug}` with
  the partner's logo + a personalised welcome (Acumen builds this in 1 day)
- Partner sends ONE intro email or Slack post to their cohort linking to it
- Acumen runs ONE 60-min "CFO clinic" Zoom for the cohort (group Q&A)
- 15% revenue share on all paid upgrades originating from the partner's
  portfolio, paid quarterly
- Cross-promotion: Acumen homepage lists partner as a featured collaborator
- **Operational lift on partner: 1 hour total**

### Tier 3 — Anchor Partnership ("for the marquee name")

- Tier 2 benefits, PLUS:
- 90-day exclusivity in their geo (we won't sign a competing accelerator
  in the same city/program category)
- Co-authored quarterly "State of Pre-Seed Finance" report — partner gets
  the data, gets to publish it under their brand, Acumen co-bylines
- Free Founder Call for every portfolio founder in their next cohort
- 20% revenue share
- Joint event: one in-person "CFO clinic" at the partner's annual summit
- Reserved for ONE partnership per market (one India, one US, one SEA)
- **Operational lift on partner: ~4 hours over the quarter**

## 5. The principles

1. **Make the partner the hero.** Every co-branded asset puts the partner's
   logo and language ahead of Acumen's. The accelerator is the trusted
   brand the founder already loves; we're the useful tool they didn't know
   they had.
2. **One email is the entire ask.** Never require the partner to do more
   than forward one email or post in one Slack. If we need them to do more,
   we've designed the partnership wrong.
3. **The founder always wins, even if they never pay us.** Free Snapshot
   has real CFO value. If we ever make a partner feel like we used them to
   harass their founders, the partnership ends in 30 days and so does
   our reputation.
4. **The economics work because of LTV, not Snapshot revenue.** We can
   afford to give Tier-1 perks for free because ~10% of partnership
   founders convert to $2-8k/month retainers later. Model the partnership
   on retainer-LTV, not Snapshot revenue.
5. **Quarterly retention review with every Tier 2/3 partner.** Show them
   the numbers — Snapshots delivered, founders helped, paid conversions,
   testimonials. Make their renewal feel like a no-brainer.

## 6. What NOT to do

- Don't pitch this to General Partners (GPs). They invest; they don't run
  founder programs. Pitch to **Platform team / Community lead / Program
  Manager / Head of Portfolio Success.**
- Don't pitch to accelerators outside the ICP (e.g. enterprise-only
  accelerators like AlchemistX won't have our founder profile).
- Don't offer Tier 3 before you've delivered Tier 1 well for a smaller
  partner. The Tier 3 "anchor" needs proof. Use the first 2–3 Tier 1
  partnerships as proof points.
- Don't price Deep Dive lower than $49 in any partnership. Free is fine;
  half-off is fine; below-half-off signals "this product has no real
  value."
- Don't agree to exclusivity that prevents you from signing partners in
  other regions. Tier 3 exclusivity is GEO-scoped only.
