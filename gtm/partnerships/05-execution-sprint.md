# 14-Day Partnership Execution Sprint

> Day-by-day plan from 2026-05-15 → 2026-05-28. Goal: 3 signed Tier 1
> partnerships and at least one Tier 2 in active negotiation by Day 14.

---

## Sprint principles

1. **Parallel pitches**, not sequential. Send 10 first-touches in Week 1;
   one of them will move faster than the others.
2. **Warm-intro work counts.** Twitter reply-guy and LinkedIn comment
   awareness are work, not procrastination. They double partnership
   reply rates.
3. **The first response is a meeting, not a yes.** Don't try to close
   in email. Always book a 20-min Zoom — that's where partnerships sign.
4. **Don't pitch all 10 partners the same template.** Personalise to the
   specific program structure (Atoms has a perks marketplace; Surge has
   curriculum; Antler has Embark; Blume has Discovery; etc.).
5. **Stop-loss: if zero Tier 1 signed by Day 14, the channel has a
   product or positioning problem.** Diagnose before scaling.

## The 20-minute partnership call structure

When a partner books a call with you, here's the structure. Don't
freelance. This sells.

**0:00 — 0:02  Set the frame**
> "Thanks for the time. I'll keep this to 20 minutes. Quick context on
> what I'm proposing, then I want to hear your two biggest concerns and
> work backwards from those. Sound good?"

**0:02 — 0:07  Show, don't tell (5 min)**
- Screen-share the Acumen homepage
- Walk through the 3-tier pricing
- Open a sample Snapshot PDF — show the actual artifact
- Show the partner-branded landing page mockup (you can build a
  static screenshot in 30 min using `partners.html`)

**0:07 — 0:12  The offer (5 min)**
- "Here's what I'm proposing for {partner-name}:" — read out the Tier 2
  benefits from `02-offer-mechanics.md`
- "Operationally on your side: one email to your cohort, that's it.
  You don't have to maintain anything."
- "Revenue share: 15% on Deep Dive, Founder Call, and the first year of
  any retainer your founders sign with us."

**0:12 — 0:17  Handle their concerns (5 min)**

Pre-built objection responses:

| Concern                                          | Response                                                                     |
|--------------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| "How do we know the CFO advice is actually good?" | Show 3 anonymised Snapshot examples + offer them a free Snapshot themselves  |
| "What about data privacy?"                       | Walk through the NDA-equivalent terms + the "never used to train models" policy |
| "What if a founder complains?"                   | Escalation path: any complaint goes to me personally within 4 hours; we'll refund any paid product on request, no questions asked |
| "We already have a finance partner / bookkeeper" | "Great — we're not replacing them. They do bookkeeping; we do CFO-level analysis. Different layer. Happy to coordinate with them on any individual founder." |
| "Can we limit how often you email our founders?" | Yes. Default: one email when Snapshot delivered, one 7-day follow-up if they don't engage. Beyond that, nothing. You set the rules. |

**0:17 — 0:20  The close**
> "Two ways this goes: (a) you tell me 'try us for one cohort, see how it
> lands' — I send you the one-pager today, you sign tomorrow, we have
> the landing page live by Friday and the cohort email goes out next
> Monday. (b) you tell me 'we need to think about it' — totally fair,
> what's the one thing I haven't answered yet that would help?"

**Post-call (within 2h):**
- Send a thank-you email that summarises everything verbally agreed +
  attaches the one-page agreement + asks for logo + brand colour

## Building the partner landing page (1 day)

`partners.html` (already committed) is the template. To build a
partner-specific version:

1. Take a screenshot of `partners.html` in a browser
2. Duplicate the file to `partners/{slug}.html` OR add `?partner={slug}`
   query parameter handling
3. Swap the partner logo image
4. Swap the partner name in the heading
5. Swap the discount code references (e.g., `BLUME50` for Blume)
6. Deploy to Vercel (auto on commit)
7. Send the partner the link

**Goal:** Partner says yes on Tuesday → landing page is live on Wednesday
→ cohort email goes out on Thursday. Speed signals competence.

## Stop-loss tripwires

| Day  | Tripwire                                       | Intervention                                              |
|------|------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| 3    | 0 replies to 6+ sent emails                    | Pause. Rewrite Email-1. Test a new subject line. Send to next batch. |
| 7    | 0 calls booked                                  | The hook is wrong. A/B test 2 hooks in next batch: (a) "free Snapshot perk", (b) "we'd love to interview your founders for our 'State of Pre-Seed Finance' report" (Trojan horse → conversation → pitch) |
| 10   | 0 verbal yeses                                  | The offer is wrong. Drop revenue-share complexity for Tier 1 — make it purely "free perk, listed, zero ask of you". Re-pitch. |
| 14   | 0 Tier 1 signed                                 | The product positioning has a fit gap. Talk to 3 platform leads at random VCs (not pitch them — *interview* them) about what perk partnerships they actually accept and why. Reposition. |

If any tripwire fires, **don't keep volume-sending.** Diagnose first.
