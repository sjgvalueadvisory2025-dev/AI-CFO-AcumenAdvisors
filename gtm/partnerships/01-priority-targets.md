## Decision-maker map — who to NEVER cold-pitch directly

| Level                       | Examples                          | Why not                                          |
|-----------------------------|-----------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Founding GPs / Managing Partners | Shailendra Singh, Karthik Reddy, Mukul Arora | They invest. They have 20+ DMs/day from operators. Won't read. |
| Senior Partners doing diligence  | Most Accel / Lightspeed / Peak XV partners | Same — wrong job function for this.            |
| Investment Associates           | n/a                                | They want to source deals, not approve perk partnerships. |

**Always pitch to:**
- Platform Manager / Director
- Head of Portfolio Success / Head of Community
- Program Director / Cohort Lead
- "Founder Experience" / "Founder Support" lead
- Communications / Marketing lead at the firm (for co-marketing
  partnerships specifically)

If you can't find a titled person in those roles, that VC firm doesn't
have institutional partnership capacity — move them down the priority list.
