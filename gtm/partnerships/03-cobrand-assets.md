# Co-Brandable Partnership Assets

> Ready-to-use copy and templates. The partner does as little work as
> possible — paste their logo, replace `{partner-name}`, hit send.

---

## A. The email the partner forwards to their cohort

This is the **single most important asset in the partnership**. Get it
right and ~30% of recipients will click. Get it wrong and the partnership
dies.

We give the partner THREE versions to choose from. They pick one.

### Version 1 — From the platform team ("operational")

```
Subject: New founder benefit — CFO support for {partner-name} founders

Hi everyone,

We just added a new benefit to the {partner-name} founder kit.

Acumen Advisors built an AI-powered CFO service specifically for
first-time, pre-Series A founders. Every report is reviewed and signed
off by a senior CFO; turnaround is 24 hours.

For {partner-name} founders, the benefit is:
- Free CFO-reviewed Snapshot (your real runway, top 3 cash leaks, and 3
  things to fix first — written in plain English)
- $49 Deep Dive (normally $99) — full unit economics, scenario planning,
  investor-ready financial story
- $261 Founder Call (normally $349) — 90-min working session with a
  senior CFO

Claim your Snapshot here: https://acumenadvisors-ai-cfo.vercel.app/partners/{partner-slug}

This is free for every {partner-name} founder. Whether you use it once
or build it into your monthly routine, hopefully it's useful.

Questions? Reply to this email.

Best,
{platform-team-name}
{partner-name}
```

## B. The first Snapshot output email (co-branded)

This is what the founder receives 24 hours after submitting. It's
co-branded so the partner gets credit and so the founder understands
*why* a stranger is giving them a free analysis.

```
Subject: Your Acumen Snapshot — {founder-first-name}, {company}

Hi {founder-first-name},

Your free Snapshot is below — delivered with thanks to the team at
{partner-name} for connecting us.

The report (attached PDF) covers:
1. Your real runway (in months, calculated honestly)
2. Top 3 cash leaks we spotted
3. Three things to fix first

A senior CFO reviewed and signed off on this report before it left
our hands.

One small ask before you go:
On a scale of 1 to 5, how soon do you expect your next raise, hire,
or pricing change? (1 = next year, 5 = this month) Just hit reply with
a number.

If you want to go further:
- $49 Deep Dive (50% off, code: {PARTNER}50) — full unit economics +
  investor-ready financial story
- $261 Founder Call (25% off, code: {PARTNER}25) — 90-min working
  session with a senior CFO

Both are at https://acumenadvisors-ai-cfo.vercel.app

Thanks again to {partner-name} for the introduction.

— {senior-cfo-name}
Acumen Advisors
```

## C. The 60-min "CFO clinic" agenda (Tier 2+)

For partners who agreed to a quarterly CFO clinic for their cohort. This
is what we run on Zoom.

```
ACUMEN × {PARTNER} CFO CLINIC
Live · 60 minutes · Zoom

Agenda:

00:00 — 05:00   Welcome from {partner platform lead}
05:00 — 20:00   "The 5 numbers every pre-Series-A founder should know
                cold" — Acumen CFO walk-through
20:00 — 50:00   Live Q&A on attendees' actual numbers (founders submit
                questions in advance; we anonymize and address up to 8)
50:00 — 55:00   How to claim your free Snapshot / Deep Dive / Founder
                Call
55:00 — 60:00   Closing & resource links

Pre-work for attendees (optional):
- Submit one specific finance question via the form (link)
- Bring your most recent month-end P&L if you want a private hot-seat
  follow-up

Tech: Zoom, recording released to cohort after.
```

## F. The "warm referral" follow-up (for Snapshots that don't convert)

The partner doesn't see this — but it's the closing motion. Sent 7 days
after a Snapshot is delivered to a non-converted founder.

```
Subject: re: your Acumen Snapshot

Hey {firstName},

Quick follow-up. We delivered your Snapshot a week ago via the
{partner-name} benefit.

Two questions:
1. Was there anything in the report you wanted us to go deeper on?
2. Anything about the Deep Dive ($49 with code {PARTNER}50) that's
   keeping you from pulling the trigger?

If the answer is "I just got busy" — totally fair, no offence taken.
If the answer is "I'm not sure the Deep Dive is worth $49 to me" — also
fair, and worth us knowing.

Either way, the offer is open with the partner discount as long as
you're a {partner-name} portfolio founder.

— {senior-cfo-name}
```

## G. The partner-logo asset request

When a partnership signs, we send the partner a short message asking
for their brand assets. Keep this dead simple.

```
Subject: assets we need for your {partner-name} landing page

Hi {firstName},

Quick logistics — to spin up your custom founder landing page
(launching in 24h), I need three things from you:

1. Your logo (SVG or 2x PNG, both light and dark backgrounds if you
   have them)
2. Your primary brand colour (hex code)
3. The exact phrase you want at the top of the page — e.g. "{Partner-Name}
   Founders" or "Welcome, {Partner} Portfolio" or anything else

Reply with these and the page is live in a day. Founders can start
claiming their free Snapshots the day after.

— {senderFirstName}
```
