# Acumen Advisors AI-CFO — Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

> Derived from the live landing page (`index.html`, `plans.html`). The product is
> a CFO-reviewed financial analysis service for founders, delivered in 24 hours,
> priced Free / $99 / $349, with the strategic intent of converting paid users
> into ongoing fractional-CFO retainers.

---

## 1. One-line ICP

**A first-time, non-finance founder of a pre-seed to Series A startup ($0–$3M
ARR, 2–30 employees) who is 3–12 months from a funding event, hiring decision,
or pricing change — and cannot yet justify a $200k/year CFO.**

## 3. Verticals (priority order)

1. **B2B SaaS / Vertical SaaS** — clearest unit-economics narrative (CAC, payback, NDR, gross margin)
2. **AI-native startups (B2B & B2C)** — burning fast, raising fast, need scenario plans
3. **D2C / E-commerce brands** — margin, cohort, inventory tied up in working capital
4. **Fintech / Insurtech / Lending** — need defensible economics for investor diligence
5. **HealthTech / DTC clinics / Telehealth** — multi-revenue-stream complexity
6. **Marketplaces** — take-rate, GMV vs. net revenue confusion
7. **Climate / DeepTech (software-led)** — long horizons, strong scenario-planning need
8. **Creator-economy & media tooling** — fast top-line, weak margin discipline

Lower priority (longer sales cycle, more bespoke):
- Hardware-heavy, biotech with IND timelines, regulated medical devices

## 6. The 5 buying triggers (target these first)

These are the moments where the prospect is most likely to convert. The
prospect list and outreach cadence are organised around these.

1. **Just raised pre-seed / seed in the last 0–60 days.** Has fresh cash, hasn't yet hired finance. Highest-intent.
2. **Currently raising / opened a round.** Needs an investor-ready financial story now.
3. **Just graduated from an accelerator (YC W26/S26, Antler, Techstars, Sequoia Surge, On Deck).** Has the deck, doesn't have the numbers.
4. **Recently posted on X/LinkedIn about runway, hiring, pricing, or "asked by an investor".** Public pain signal.
5. **Hired their 5th, 10th, or 20th employee in the last 30 days.** Burn just jumped; runway just shrank.

## 7. Anti-ICP (do NOT pursue)

- Companies with an existing CFO, VP Finance, or full-time Controller
- Series B+ funded (>$15M raised) — they will buy retainers, not $99 reports
- Pre-revenue ideation stage with no bank balance to analyse
- Regulated banks, public companies, government — wrong buying motion
- Solo consultants / freelancers — too small to need this
- Crypto/web3 protocols without a corp entity — compliance complexity

## 8. Pricing psychology (informs targeting)

- **Free Snapshot** is the wedge. Goal: get them to upload P&L + bank statements.
- **$99 Deep Dive** is the "validate this is real" moment. Conversion target: 15–25% of Snapshots.
- **$349 Founder Call** is the high-intent buyer (raising in <90 days, or just hit a wall). Conversion target: 5–10% of Snapshots.
- **Real money is downstream:** of the founders who buy $349, ~10–20% will be candidates for ongoing fractional-CFO retainers at $2k–$8k/month. THAT is the LTV bet.

**Conservatively addressable ICP universe: ~6,000–8,000 companies globally.**
Plenty of room. Targeting 100–150 for the first cycle is correct: depth over breadth.

## 10. Why this ICP closes

The product is engineered for this exact buyer:

- **24-hour turnaround** removes the "I don't have time to brief a CFO" objection
- **Free Snapshot** removes the "what if this isn't useful" objection
- **CFO-reviewed-and-signed-off** removes the "I don't trust AI with my numbers" objection
- **Plain English** removes the "I don't understand finance jargon" objection
- **Three explicit fit-stories** on `plans.html` ("I just want to know if I'm okay" / "I'm about to raise/hire/re-price" / "I need to talk this through") map to three real buying moments
- **$99 / $349 price** sits below every approval threshold a founder has

When you put this in front of a founder who *just raised pre-seed and is being
asked by their lead investor for a financial model*, the conversion is
mechanical.
