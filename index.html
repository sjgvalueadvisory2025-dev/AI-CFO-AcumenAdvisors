02 — The Case WHY CFO-LED

Most founder decisions are financial decisions in disguise.

Hiring, pricing, fundraising, equity splits — the surface is operational. The substance is capital allocation.

01 / Capital Discipline

Every dollar has an opportunity cost

A CFO-led founder asks which dollar buys the most defensible asset — and benchmarks it against the alternative.

02 / Runway Truth

Burn is not runway

Most early companies overstate runway by 2–4 months. That's a fatal margin of error.

03 / Unit Economics

Growth without unit economics is debt

If contribution margin is negative, every new customer makes the company worse, not better.

04 / Investor Narrative

Investors fund frameworks, not feelings

The same business, pitched with and without a coherent model, closes at very different valuations.

05 / Pricing Power

Pricing is your highest-leverage variable

A 1% price improvement beats a 1% volume improvement by 4–8x in operating profit.

06 / Defensible Diligence

Survive the diligence room

Clean financials and a defensible model preserve valuation when the term sheet arrives.
